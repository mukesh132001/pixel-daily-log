Privacy

Pixel daily log is a small student project. Here’s exactly what it keeps.

Without signing in

Everything stays in your own browser: your profile, unlocks, streak log, hand-logged runs and, if you connect it, your Strava login. Nothing about you is sent to us.

If you sign in

Signing in is optional. It lets you open your profile on another device. Your account stores:

  • Your email address, to send you sign-in links.
  • Your profile: name, the line about you, school name, initials and colors, your GitHub username (if you added one), the character you picked and your pet.
  • Your unlocks: which characters you’ve earned and the date.
  • Your streak log: the dates you logged a commit or a run, and your best streak.
  • Your room’s XP: one number per day for about the last two months (older days are added into a single total), the last room level you were shown, and the year you last saw each seasonal greeting (like “Happy Halloween!”).

It never stores your Strava login, your Strava runs or your hand-logged runs; those stay in the browser where you added them.

Accounts are run by Supabase, which hosts the database and sends the sign-in emails. The database only lets each person read and change their own saved profile.

Deleting your data

  • Your saved profile: sign in, open Account, and choose Delete my saved profile. It’s removed right away (your browser keeps its own copy until you clear it).
  • Your email and account: ask Muki, who runs Pixel daily log, through github.com/mukesh132001, and it will be deleted along with anything still saved.
  • This browser’s copy: clear this site’s data in your browser settings.
